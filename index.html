DIY Fling Before the Ring Decor

Last Fling Before the Ring Decor Checklist

Decorating yourself? Pack these — Charleston humidity is tough on balloons and adhesive, so a few extras go a long way.

🎈 Walls & Backdrops

  • Balloon garland kit + strip tape
  • Fringe or tinsel curtain
  • "Fling Before the Ring" banner
  • Command hooks (rental-safe)
  • Hand pump + glue dots

🥂 Table & Bar

  • Themed napkins, plates, and cups
  • Bride and crew straws
  • Ring-shaped ice molds
  • Champagne flute charms
  • Confetti for the tablescape

💍 Bride Extras

  • Sash, veil, or pearl headband
  • Balloon "BRIDE" letters for the bedroom
  • Welcome bags for the crew
  • Photo props and a selfie light
  • Steamer for outfits

Humidity tip: Charleston summers wilt latex balloons fast. Keep garlands indoors with the AC on, inflate the day of, and ask your balloon vendor about treated balloons for outdoor setups on porches or docks. Most rentals don't allow tape on painted walls, so plan for hooks or freestanding backdrop frames.
