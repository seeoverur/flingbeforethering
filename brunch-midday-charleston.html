What Is a Charleston Midday Brunch Show?

A Charleston midday brunch show is a private, in-home entertainment experience that arrives at your vacation rental, beach house, or private venue during late morning or early afternoon. Instead of fighting for a brunch reservation table at a packed King Street restaurant on a Saturday, your group stays in your space — whether that's a Wild Dunes villa on Isle of Palms, a Center Street Folly Beach rental, a downtown Charleston Airbnb in Cannonborough-Elliotborough, or a Mount Pleasant vacation home near Shem Creek — and the entertainment comes to you.

It's ideal for the window between late morning and early evening: after everyone's had coffee and before the nightlife energy kicks in. Groups typically pair the experience with catered food, mimosas from Total Wine on Sam Rittenberg Boulevard or Harris Teeter on Coleman Boulevard in Mount Pleasant, and a playlist that gets the afternoon started right.

The result is a memorable, completely personalized celebration that doesn't require reservations, Ubers, or heels at noon — and it creates the kind of group energy that carries into the rest of the night.
