Charleston Bachelorette Guide

Charleston, South Carolina — the Holy City — is one of the most consistently ranked bachelorette destinations on the East Coast. With Folly Beach, Isle of Palms, and Sullivan's Island within 30 minutes, a walkable King Street lined with boutiques and rooftop bars, and a Lowcountry dining scene loaded with James Beard–recognized restaurants, it delivers the full spectrum: romantic mornings, beach afternoons, and nights that get memorable fast.

Explore the Charleston Guide

Why Charleston Works

The Holy City is compact enough to be walkable but packed enough to fill three full days without repeating yourself. Fly into Charleston International Airport (CHS) off Aviation Avenue in North Charleston and you're 20 minutes from downtown, 35 minutes from Folly Beach via SC-171, and about 30 minutes from Isle of Palms via the IOP Connector off US-17.

The downtown peninsula — bounded by the Ashley River to the west and the Cooper River to the east — keeps everything walkable. Upper King Street handles shopping and cocktail bars. The French Quarter handles fine dining. East Bay Street handles waterfront views and Rainbow Row photos. Market Street handles the City Market and late-night spots. It's all within about a mile of each other, which means no one's wasting the night in an Uber.

Whether the bride wants low-key Lowcountry vibes or a full-tilt girls weekend, the city bends to fit. That's the secret to why Charleston keeps showing up on every bachelorette destination list — it genuinely works for every group type.

A Smart Charleston Bachelorette Formula

Morning

Brunch on the Peninsula

Poogan's Porch on Queen Street, Miller's All Day on King, or a live brunch show option near the Market Street corridor.

Afternoon

Beach, Shopping, or a Workshop

Candle-making at Candlefish on King Street, a boutique crawl from Upper King to the French Quarter, or sand at Isle of Palms Front Beach.

Evening

Dinner + Rooftop Cocktails

Reserve early at FIG, Husk on Queen, or Slightly North of Broad on East Bay — then rooftop drinks at The Watch on Wentworth or the Vendue.

Night

Private Show or Live Music

A private entertainer at your rental, or live music and dancing near Upper King Street or the Market Street nightlife corridor.

Candle Making – Candlefish on King Street

Candlefish on upper King Street offers private BYOB candle-making workshops for groups up to 20. Choose from 100+ scents and pour custom candles to take home. Tijon Fragrance Lab on middle King does custom perfume blending for a more personal memento.

King Street Boutique Crawl

Work your way from Kenny Flowers at 345 King Street (rooftop Cabana Club rentable for groups), south through Savannah Bee Co. for mead tastings, into the French Quarter boutiques near Market Street. The King Street shopping tour starting at The Watch on Wentworth Street adds guided wine stops and boutique discounts.

Beach Day – Folly, IOP, or Sullivan's

Three beaches, three personalities. Folly Beach off Folly Road (SC-171) is funky and local — check for weekend music festivals. Isle of Palms via the IOP Connector is resort-easy with calmer water. Sullivan's Island is serene with low-rise beach bars near Station 22½.

Folly Beach Isle of Palms Sullivan's Island
📸

Rainbow Row & The Battery Walk

The iconic 13 pastel Georgian houses at 83–107 East Bay Street, then south to Waterfront Park's pineapple fountain, The Battery seawall, and a picnic under live oaks at White Point Gardens. Entirely free. Best before 9am for golden-hour light and zero crowds.

East Bay Street Waterfront Park White Point Gardens Free
⛵

Shem Creek Afternoon & Sunset Sail

Cross the Arthur Ravenel Jr. Bridge into Mount Pleasant for waterfront drinks at Tavern & Table or Red's Ice House on Shem Creek. The Palmetto Breeze catamaran departs from Shem Creek for 2-hour BYOB sunset sails — one of the most genuinely memorable low-key bach experiences in the area.

🌙 Evening & Nightlife
🍽️

Dinner in the French Quarter or South of Broad

Book early at Husk on Queen Street, FIG on Meeting Street (James Beard–winning Sean Brock territory), or Slightly North of Broad (SNOB) on East Bay. For a Victorian townhouse feel, Poogan's Porch on Queen Street has a massive wine list. Reserve 3–4 weeks ahead for weekend nights.

Rooftop Cocktails Before The Night Starts

Hit The Rooftop at the Vendue on Vendue Range (harbor views, incredible sunsets), The Watch rooftop on Wentworth Street for champagne and truffle fries, or Hotel Bennett's rooftop pool deck on King Street if you're staying there. Rooftops are ideal for the 7–9pm window before nightlife kicks in.

For the group that wants energy without a crowded club, Michael At Your Place brings a private show directly to your rental — Isle of Palms beach house, Folly Beach vacation rental, or downtown Charleston Airbnb. Ghost Tour – Old South Carriage or Bulldog Tours

Old South Carriage on Anson Street runs evening ghost carriage rides through Ansonborough and Harleston Village. Bulldog Tours on North Market Street covers the Church Street corridor and City Market area on foot. 90 minutes, no walking stamina required — perfect in heels.

Live Music & Dancing on Upper King

The Upper King Street corridor between Calhoun and Spring is the beating heart of Charleston nightlife. The Commodore delivers vintage-cool cocktails and live music. Prohibition near Market Street goes late with DJs. For something more current, check what's on at local live music venues and event spaces around the North Morrison corridor in NoMo — programming changes fast, so verify ahead.

2-Night Itinerary

Built around a Wild Dunes or downtown Airbnb base. Adjust beach vs. city balance to taste.

Friday

Arrive at CHS, check into your Isle of Palms rental near Palm Boulevard or a downtown vacation home on Tradd Street or Broad Street. Welcome drinks at the rental, then dinner at Husk on Queen Street — book this reservation weeks in advance. End the night with rooftop drinks at The Rooftop at the Vendue on Vendue Range overlooking Charleston Harbor.

Saturday Morning

Brunch at Miller's All Day on King Street or the live brunch show option near Market Street. Walk off brunch on a Rainbow Row and Battery stroll starting at East Bay Street.

Saturday Afternoon

Candle-making at Candlefish on King Street (BYOB), followed by a shopping crawl toward the French Quarter. Or swap for a Folly Beach afternoon if the weather is perfect.

Saturday Night

Dinner at FIG on Meeting Street or Slightly North of Broad on East Bay, then the highlight of the weekend: a private show from Michael At Your Place at your rental, or a ghost carriage tour with Old South Carriage on Anson Street before hitting Upper King Street bars.

Sunday

Recovery brunch, a final stroll through the Charleston City Market on Market Street for sweetgrass baskets and souvenirs, then head to CHS for flights home. Check the birthday ideas page if anyone in the group has a milestone coming up — several of these activities double perfectly.

Where to Stay

Downtown (smaller groups of 4–8): Airbnbs near Upper King, Cannonborough-Elliotborough, or Harleston Village put you within walking distance of everything. Hotel Bennett on King Street has a rooftop pool and is walking distance to every restaurant on this list.

Beach stays (larger groups of 10–15): Isle of Palms rentals near Front Beach on Palm Boulevard or Wild Dunes villas for more space. Uber to downtown runs about 25–30 minutes. Folly Beach rentals near Center Street are closer to downtown (~20 min) and have a cooler, more local vibe.

Planning Tips

Best weather: March–May and September–November. Summer works but expect heat and humidity — schedule beach time early, evenings downtown.

Book restaurant reservations 3–4 weeks ahead for weekend nights, especially FIG, Husk, and Poogan's Porch.

If your group includes guests under 21, verify each venue's entry policy directly — nightlife rules vary by event, night, and venue type.
