Your Local Resource for Charleston Trips

Welcome to Fling Before The Ring — a curated activity guide built specifically for groups visiting the Holy City of Charleston, South Carolina. Whether you're a maid of honor planning a bachelorette weekend, a best friend organizing a milestone birthday, or a group of women putting together a girls trip in the Lowcountry, this guide is built for you.

Charleston keeps earning its spot on every best bachelorette destination list in the country — and it's not hard to see why. The downtown peninsula bounded by the Ashley River and Cooper River puts world-class dining, boutique shopping on King Street, historic landmarks like Rainbow Row on East Bay Street, and a buzzing nightlife corridor all within walking distance of each other. Add in Folly Beach off SC-171, Isle of Palms via the IOP Connector, Sullivan's Island, and the Shem Creek waterfront in Mount Pleasant — all reachable in under 30 minutes — and you have one of the most well-rounded girls weekend destinations on the East Coast.

Our mission is simple: cut through the generic travel lists and give you the kind of local detail that actually helps you plan — specific street addresses, neighborhood context, booking tips, and honest assessments of what works for groups.

Bachelorette Party Activities — From low key last fling before the ring ideas like candle making at Candlefish on King Street and carriage ghost tours with Old South Carriage on Anson Street, to private entertainment that comes directly to your rental through Michael At Your Place.

Brunch & Midday Entertainment — The Charleston midday brunch show is one of the most unique daytime activities available for girls groups — a private in-home experience that works for rentals across the entire Lowcountry, from downtown French Quarter Airbnbs to Wild Dunes villas on Isle of Palms.

Birthday Girls Night Out — Dinner at Halls Chophouse on King Street, dancing at Republic Garden & Lounge at 462 King, a self-guided King Street bar crawl from Uptown Social at 587 King south to the Calhoun Street intersection, and private entertainment options. See all Charleston birthday ideas.

Full Charleston Bachelorette Weekend Planning — A complete Charleston bachelorette guide covering the whole weekend: where to stay (Hotel Bennett on King Street vs. Isle of Palms beach rentals), where to eat (FIG on Meeting Street, Husk on Queen Street, Poogan's Porch), rooftop bars (The Vendue on Vendue Range), and the best nightlife corridor on Upper King Street between Calhoun and Spring.
